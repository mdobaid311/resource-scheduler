# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) in a shorter form.

## Our standard

Be kind and professional. Assume good faith, give specific and respectful feedback, accept it gracefully, and remember that people here are volunteers working in their own time. Everyone is welcome regardless of background, identity or experience level.

Not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks
- sexualized language or unwelcome attention
- publishing someone's private information
- spam, or promoting unrelated products in issues and discussions
- anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the email address on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/mdobaid311), or use a private security advisory if the matter is sensitive. Reports are handled in confidence.
