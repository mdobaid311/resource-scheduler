## What you get

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Move, resize, create" icon="random">
    Pointer-based drag and drop for mouse, pen and touch. No drag-and-drop library to wire up, and no clash with one you already use.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Conflict rules" icon="approve-check">
    Forbid overlaps or write your own rule. Rejected places show in red before you drop, for drag, resize and create alike.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your own dialogs" icon="pencil">
    `onSlotSelect` hands you the range; you open your own form. Day view slots of 15, 30 or 60 minutes, with visible hours.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keyboard and screen readers" icon="open-book">
    An ARIA grid with named cells, keyboard move, resize and create, and live announcements. Checked with axe in CI.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Themeable, and isolated" icon="setting">
    Design tokens are CSS variables and every rule is scoped, so importing it never restyles your app.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Copy the source" icon="laptop">
    Prefer to own the code? The shadcn CLI copies it into your project and adds the styles to your Tailwind CSS.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
